# sources/ — documents cited

The PDFs and HTML pages in this folder are the copies consulted while writing the papers, kept
so that page references can be checked. They are **not tracked in git** (the EIR modeling appendix
alone is 113 MB, and the documents belong to their publishers). The `.txt` files are plain-text
extractions used for searching; they are not tracked either. Everything below is public and can
be re-downloaded from the links given.
